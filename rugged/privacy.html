INTRODUCTION

Welcome to Rugged App (“we,” “our,” “us”). We are committed to safeguarding your privacy and ensuring that your personal data is protected. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal data when you use our services.

1. WHO WE ARE

Rugged App is a digital tracking tool for High Intensity Training workout logging.

2. DATA WE COLLECT

We collect account data (email, name) and training data (workout logs, volume, strength metrics) that you voluntarily input to deliver sync and analytics services.

3. HOW WE USE YOUR DATA

We use your data solely to provide, maintain, and synchronize your workout logs across devices.

4. YOUR RIGHTS

You have the right to access, export, or delete your account data at any time inside the app settings.

5. DATA SECURITY

We implement industry-standard encryption and security protocols to safeguard your information.
