v1.1.0 September 2026

ADAPTIVE ARCHITECTURE & OFFLINE-FIRST SYNC

This release introduces significant structural enhancements, focusing on device compatibility and data reliability across the training ecosystem.

  • Adaptive Layout Engine: Implementation of intelligent UI scaling for Tablets and Foldables. The system dynamically adjusts font sizes, widget dimensions, and navigation components.
  • Offline-First Sync Architecture: A robust overhaul of the data synchronization layer with local SQLite persistence and background reconciliation to the cloud.
v1.0.0 August 2026

INITIAL DEPLOYMENT

The deployment of Rugged App as a digital ecosystem for High Intensity Training and physiological monitoring.

  • HIT Library: Digital archive comprising classic High Intensity Training cycles and workouts.
  • Exercise Suite: Dedicated exercise manager providing access to movement guides and custom exercise tracking.
  • The Tracker: Centralized tracking modules for HIT Workouts, Body Composition, Nutrition, Hydration, Sleep, and Supplement Stacks.
